## Risk Mitigation

### If Outbound Isn't Working
**Problem:** < 1% reply rate on cold outreach
**Solutions:**
- Rewrite messaging (test 3-5 variants)
- Change target audience (different industry/size)
- Switch channel (try Twitter/X instead of LinkedIn)
- Add more personalization (reference recent news about company)

### If Website Traffic Is Low
**Problem:** < 100 visitors/week after Month 1
**Solutions:**
- Increase content output (3 posts/week)
- Guest post on established blogs
- Appear on podcasts (pitch 20 shows)
- Run paid ads to drive initial traffic
- Post in communities (Indie Hackers, Reddit, etc.)

### If Calls Aren't Converting
**Problem:** < 20% close rate on proposals
**Solutions:**
- Improve qualification (better discovery calls)
- Add more social proof (case studies, testimonials)
- Adjust pricing (offer payment plans)
- Create lower-tier entry offer ($5K pilot project)
- Improve proposal quality (more specific ROI)

### If Cash Flow Is Tight
**Problem:** Can't sustain ad spend or hiring
**Solutions:**
- Focus on organic channels (content, outbound)
- Require 50% upfront on all projects
- Offer discount for upfront annual payment
- Start with VA before full-time hires
- Use commission-only SDR initially
