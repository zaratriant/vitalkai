## Discovery Call Script

### Opening (2 min)
```
"Thanks for making time, {{first_name}}. 

To make the best use of our 30 minutes, here's what I'm hoping to do:
1. Understand your current operations and biggest pain points
2. Identify 2-3 specific automation opportunities
3. If there's a fit, explore what working together might look like

How does that sound?

Great. Let's dive in - can you walk me through a typical week in your role 
and what takes up most of your time?"
```

### Discovery Questions (15 min)

**Current State:**
- "What's the most repetitive task you or your team does weekly?"
- "Where do you feel like you're constantly putting out fires?"
- "What tools/software are you using? What's frustrating about them?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand and automate one thing, what would it be?"

**Impact:**
- "How much time would you estimate that takes per week?"
- "What's the cost of getting that wrong? (errors, delays, customer complaints)"
- "How does this bottleneck affect other parts of the business?"

**Previous Attempts:**
- "Have you tried to automate this before? What happened?"
- "What's stopped you from solving this until now?"

**Budget & Authority:**
- "Have you invested in automation or process improvement before? What was the budget range?"
- "Who else would be involved in a decision like this?"
- "If we could solve this, what would the timeline look like on your end?"

### Transition (5 min)
```
"Based on what you've shared, I can already see 2-3 opportunities that could 
have a real impact:

1. [Opportunity 1] - This alone could save you X hours/week
2. [Opportunity 2] - This would eliminate the [specific pain] you mentioned
3. [Opportunity 3] - This is a quick win we could deliver in week 1

Does this align with what you're seeing?"
```

### Close for Next Step (5 min)
```
"Here's what I'd recommend:

I'll put together a proposal outlining:
- The specific automations we'd build
- Timeline (typically 2-4 weeks for something like this)
- Investment (usually in the $XK-$XK range for this scope)
- Expected ROI

I can have that to you within 48 hours. Then we can hop on another call 
to answer any questions and decide if it makes sense to move forward.

Does that work for you?

Great. Any specific questions for me before we wrap?"
```

## Objection Handling Scripts

### "It's too expensive"
```
"I totally get that. Can I ask - compared to what?

Is it the total number, or are you unsure about the ROI?

Because here's the thing: you mentioned this is costing you about 20 hours/week. 
At your rate (or your team's rate), that's roughly $XK/month. This project 
pays for itself in 6-8 weeks.

So the real question is: can you afford NOT to do this?

That said, if cash flow is the issue, we could structure it as:
- 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
- Or start with a smaller pilot project to prove value

What would work better for you?"
```

---

### "I need to think about it"
```
"Of course. What specifically do you need to think through?

Is it:
- Whether this will actually work? (we can address that with references)
- Whether now is the right time? (what would need to change?)
- Whether there's a better solution? (let's compare options)
- Something else?

The more specific you can be, the better I can help."
```

---

### "I need to talk to my partner/board/team"
```
"Absolutely, that makes total sense.

Who else needs to be involved? And what questions do you think they'll have?

I'd be happy to join that conversation and answer any technical questions. 
Would it help if I put together a one-pager you can share with them?

Also - what's your timeline for making a decision?"
```

---

### "We can do this in-house"
```
"That's a fair point. You definitely could.

A few things to consider:
1. Opportunity cost - what else could your team be working on?
2. Expertise - we've done this 50+ times, so we'll avoid the pitfalls
3. Speed - we can deliver in 3-4 weeks vs. months of internal dev time

But I get it - building internal capability is valuable long-term.

What if we did a hybrid? We handle the complex stuff, your team learns 
alongside us, and you own it going forward.

Would that work better?"
```

---

### "Send me more information"
```
"Happy to. What specifically would be most helpful?

- Case studies from similar companies?
- Technical documentation on how we'd build this?
- ROI calculator you can play with?
- References from past clients?

The more specific you can be, the better I can tailor what I send."
```
