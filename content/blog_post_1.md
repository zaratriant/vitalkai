## 1. Customer Onboarding

### The Problem
New clients/customers need information collected, accounts set up, welcome emails sent, kickoff calls scheduled, and project files created. Your team does this manually every single time.

### Time Wasted
**5-10 hours per new client** × number of clients per month

### The Automation
1. Client fills out smart intake form
2. Data automatically validated and stored
3. Welcome email sequence triggered
4. Project template created in PM tool
5. Kickoff call auto-scheduled
6. Internal team notified

### Tools Needed
- Form tool (Typeform, HubSpot Forms)
- Automation platform (Make, Zapier)
- Project management (Asana, Monday, ClickUp)
- Email (Gmail, Outlook, or ESP)
- Scheduling (Calendly, Cal.com)

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 80-90% reduction
- **Error reduction:** Near-zero mistakes
- **Client satisfaction:** Faster onboarding = happier clients
- **Payback period:** 2-4 weeks

## 2. Lead Qualification & Routing

### The Problem
Marketing generates leads, but your sales team wastes time on unqualified prospects. Manual review takes hours. Good leads go cold while you're sorting through the noise.

### Time Wasted
**10-15 hours per week** on lead review and manual follow-up

### The Automation
1. Lead submits inquiry form
2. AI scores based on: budget, timeline, company size, intent signals
3. Hot leads → Instant SMS/email to sales rep
4. Warm leads → Nurture email sequence
5. Cold leads → Polite disqualification with resources
6. All data synced to CRM automatically

### Tools Needed
- Form tool with conditional logic
- AI scoring (OpenAI API, custom model)
- CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce)
- Automation platform
- Email/SMS notifications

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 10-15 hours/week
- **Response time:** Minutes instead of hours
- **Close rate:** 2-3x improvement
- **Payback period:** 1-2 weeks

---

## 3. Invoice Processing & Accounts Payable

### The Problem
Invoices arrive via email as PDFs. Your team manually extracts vendor info, amounts, and line items. Data gets entered into accounting software. Approvals happen over email. Payments are scheduled manually.

### Time Wasted
**10-20 hours per week** depending on volume

### The Automation
1. Invoices arrive at dedicated email address
2. AI extracts: vendor, amount, date, line items, PO numbers
3. Data pushed to QuickBooks/Xero automatically
4. Approval workflow triggered (only for amounts > threshold)
5. Payment auto-scheduled based on vendor terms
6. Vendor notified of payment date

### Tools Needed
- Dedicated inbox for invoices
- Document AI (OpenAI Vision, Google Document AI, specialized tools)
- Accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero)
- Automation platform
- Approval tool (or built into accounting software)

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 85-95% reduction
- **Error reduction:** <1% vs. 5% manual error rate
- **Early payment discounts:** Never miss a 2/10 net 30 discount
- **Payback period:** 4-6 weeks

## 4. Customer Support Triage

### The Problem
Support inbox is flooded with questions. 70% are repetitive (pricing, features, billing). Your team answers each one manually. Response times are slow. Customer satisfaction suffers.

### Time Wasted
**15-25 hours per week** on repetitive inquiries

### The Automation
1. Support email monitored by AI
2. Common questions answered instantly with accurate responses
3. Complex issues routed to appropriate team member
4. Priority scoring based on customer tier and issue severity
5. Auto-escalation for VIP customers or urgent issues
6. All interactions logged in help desk system

### Tools Needed
- Shared inbox (Help Scout, Zendesk, Front)
- AI response system (custom or tools like Intercom Fin)
- Automation platform
- CRM integration for customer data

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 60-80% of support volume
- **Response time:** Instant for common questions
- **Customer satisfaction:** Faster answers = happier customers
- **Payback period:** 3-5 weeks

## 5. Sales Follow-Up Sequences

### The Problem
Sales reps forget to follow up. Leads go cold. Deals slip through the cracks. Manual tracking in spreadsheets is error-prone. Revenue is left on the table.

### Time Wasted
**5-10 hours per week** on manual follow-up tracking + lost deals

### The Automation
1. New lead added to CRM
2. Multi-touch sequence automatically triggered:
   - Day 0: Immediate personalized email
   - Day 2: Follow-up with case study
   - Day 5: Value-add content share
   - Day 10: "Should I close your file?" email
   - Day 15: Final check-in
3. Any reply → Sequence pauses, rep notified
4. All activity logged in CRM
5. Lead score updated based on engagement

### Tools Needed
- CRM with automation (HubSpot, Pipedrive)
- Email automation (built-in or tools like Instantly, Smartlead)
- Calendar integration for meeting booking
- Tracking for email opens/clicks

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 5-10 hours/week per rep
- **Revenue impact:** 20-40% more deals closed
- **Consistency:** Every lead gets proper follow-up
- **Payback period:** 1-2 weeks

## 7. Contract & Document Processing

### The Problem
Contracts, NDAs, proposals arrive via email. Your team manually reviews, extracts key terms, stores in folders, and tracks renewal dates. Things get lost. Deadlines are missed.

### Time Wasted
**5-10 hours per week** on document handling

### The Automation
1. Documents arrive at dedicated inbox
2. AI extracts: parties, dates, values, key clauses, renewal terms
3. Data stored in structured database
4. Documents filed in correct folders (Drive, Dropbox, etc.)
5. Renewal/expiration alerts sent 60/30/7 days before
6. Key terms searchable in central repository

### Tools Needed
- Document AI (specialized tools or custom with OpenAI)
- Cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, Box)
- Database or contract management system
- Automation platform
- Calendar/alert system

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 70-85% reduction
- **Risk reduction:** Never miss a renewal or deadline
- **Searchability:** Find any contract term in seconds
- **Payback period:** 4-8 weeks

## 8. Employee Onboarding & Offboarding

### The Problem
New hires need accounts created, equipment ordered, training scheduled, paperwork completed. Offboarding requires access revoked, equipment returned, knowledge transferred. Manual checklists are error-prone.

### Time Wasted
**3-5 hours per employee** × number of hires/departures

### The Automation
**Onboarding:**
1. Offer accepted → Onboarding workflow triggered
2. IT tickets auto-created (laptop, accounts, access)
3. Welcome email sent with first-week schedule
4. Training modules assigned in LMS
5. 30/60/90-day check-ins scheduled
6. Manager notified of completion milestones

**Offboarding:**
1. Resignation entered → Offboarding workflow triggered
2. Access revocation scheduled for last day
3. Equipment return checklist created
4. Exit interview scheduled
5. Knowledge transfer tasks assigned
6. Final paycheck processed

### Tools Needed
- HRIS system (BambooHR, Gusto, Rippling)
- IT ticketing system
- Automation platform
- LMS for training
- E-signature for paperwork

### Expected ROI
- **Time saved:** 60-80% reduction
- **Compliance:** Never miss a required step
- **Experience:** New hires feel welcomed and prepared
- **Payback period:** 2-4 weeks (or first hire)

## How to Use This Checklist

### Step 1: Audit Your Current State
For each workflow above, rate your current state:
- 🔴 Manual (done entirely by humans)
- 🟡 Partial (some automation, but gaps remain)
- 🟢 Automated (runs without human intervention)

### Step 2: Calculate the Cost
For each 🔴 or 🟡 item:
1. How many hours per week does this take?
2. What's the fully-loaded hourly cost of the people doing it?
3. Multiply: Hours × Rate × 52 weeks = Annual cost

### Step 3: Prioritize by ROI
Rank opportunities by:
1. **Impact** (hours saved × cost per hour)
2. **Feasibility** (how hard is it to automate?)
3. **Strategic value** (does this improve customer experience?)

### Step 4: Start Small
Pick ONE workflow. Automate it completely. Measure results. Learn. Then move to the next.

Don't try to boil the ocean.

### Step 5: Track and Share
Document the results. Share wins with your team. Build momentum.
